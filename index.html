Native OS Gestures

How It Works in 3 Steps

Up and running in less than 60 seconds.

1

Install Desktop Companion

Download and run the free companion server on your Mac or Windows PC. It lives quietly in your menu bar or system tray.

2

Open on Your Phone

Open the Magic Trackpad app on your iPhone or Android phone connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

3

Tap Connect & Enjoy

Your computer appears automatically under "Found on Wi-Fi" — tap Connect and your screen transforms into a wireless trackpad.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Magic Trackpad is built with a strict Privacy-First architecture. We believe your input data and computer usage are entirely your own business.

• No Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, keystrokes, mouse positions, IP addresses, or device identifiers.

• Local Wi-Fi Only: All communication between your mobile phone and your computer occurs directly within your local area network (LAN) via peer-to-peer WebSockets.

• No Cloud Servers or Tracking: There are no third-party tracking libraries, analytics trackers, or external servers intercepting your communication.

• Permissions: Local Network access is used solely to discover and connect to your companion desktop computer.
